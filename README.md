# GelPoint

The temperature jam must reach to set, for your altitude, plus jar processing minutes. Or start from your thermometer in boiling water.

- Live: https://ilanis-agent.github.io/gelpoint/
- App: https://ilanis-agent.github.io/gelpoint/app.html

Sources: NCHFP (University of Georgia), temperature test table for jelly without added pectin: 220, 218, 216, 214, 212, 211, 209, 207, 205 F at 0 to 8,000 ft; values between rows are interpolated. UC ANR: gel point = boiling point of water + 8 F. Process times from the NCHFP jam table (half-pint and pint jars, hot pack): 5 min to 1,000 ft, 10 min to 6,000 ft, 15 min above. The table steps are rounded (one step is 1 F, not 2), so it differs from the plain 2 F per 1,000 ft rule by up to 1 F. Follow a tested recipe for canning times and acidity.

Run tests: `node test-engine.js` (53 checks).

GelPoint
The classic 220 F is only right at sea level.
Open the app
At 3,000 ft your jam is done at 214 F (101.1 C), not 220 F. Jar processing there is 10 minutes.

What it does

  • Gel temperature for any altitude from 0 to 8,000 ft (or metres)
  • Boiling-water calibration: your own thermometer reading plus 8 F
  • How many degrees are left on your jam thermometer
  • Jar processing minutes for your altitude
Table from NCHFP (University of Georgia) and the 8 F rule from UC ANR. Values between table rows are interpolated. Follow a tested recipe for canning. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source

GelPoint

The temperature your jam needs to reach, for where you live.
Sources: NCHFP (University of Georgia), temperature test table for jelly without added pectin: 220 F at sea level, 218 at 1,000 ft, 216, 214, 212, 211, 209, 207, 205 at 8,000 ft; values between are interpolated. UC ANR: the gel point is 8 F above the boiling point of water. Process times from the NCHFP jam table for half-pint and pint jars, hot pack: 5 min up to 1,000 ft, 10 min to 6,000 ft, 15 min above. Altitude changes with weather too, so the most reliable method is to boil water in the same pot and add 8 F (4.4 C). Always follow a tested recipe for canning times and acidity.
